--- [ PHASE 3 : INVENTORY STOCK & LEDGER ENGINE ] ---
[✓] C8. T15: Inventory Store State Master Model & Reorder Engine (`/api/inventory`)
    - Mongoose Model `Inventory` tracking `availableStock`, `reorderLevel`, `reservedStock`, and auto-calculated `lowStockAlert`.
    - `GET /api/inventory/summary` KPI metrics and `GET /api/inventory/store-state` real-time stock listings.

[✓] C9. T16: Inventory Ledger Schema Model & Atomic Movement Engine (`/api/inventory/ledger`)
    - Core Rule: "Never overwrite stock directly. All stock movements written as ledger logs."
    - Model `InventoryLedger` with `qtyChange`, `balanceAfter`, `type` (`IN`, `OUT`, `ADJUST`, `OPENING`, `RETURN`), and `reason`.
    - Mathematical invariant: `availableStock === sum(all ledger qtyChanges)`.

[✓] C10. T17: Opening Stock Initialization API (`POST /api/inventory/opening-stock`)
    - Official starting physical balance initialization creating an audited `OPENING` ledger entry.

[✓] C11. T18: Stock IN (Addition) Endpoint (`POST /api/inventory/stock-in`)
    - Positive inventory increment with supplier name, unit cost, reference number (PO#/Bill#), and auto-resolved alert check.

[✓] C12. T19: Stock OUT (Reduction) Endpoint (`POST /api/inventory/stock-out`, `POST /api/inventory/stock-out/batch`)
    - Negative stock deduction with strict `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` rejection (400) preventing negative stock.
    - Batch deduction endpoint for POS checkout carts.

[✓] C13. T20: Stock Reconciliation / Adjust API (`POST /api/inventory/adjust`)
    - Physical store count reconciliation against system stock with discrepancy delta calculation and reason tags.

[✓] C14. T21: Inventory Ledger Auditing Log UI & Stock Flow Statement Engine (`GET /api/inventory/ledger`)
    - Bank-statement-style chronological audit trail detailing WHO, WHEN, WHY, and WHAT changed stock levels.
    - Multi-filter toolbar (type, source, date range, search) and 1-click CSV export.

[✓] C15. T22: Low-Stock Limit Notifications & Deterministic Alerts Queue Engine (`/api/inventory/alerts`)
    - Real-time alert generation when `availableStock <= reorderLevel`.
    - Severity differentiation (`CRITICAL` for OOS, `WARNING` for Low Stock), deduplication guarantee, and replenishment auto-resolution.

[✓] C16. T23: Sale Transaction Schema Model & Invoicing Header Engine (`src/models/invoice.model.js`)
    - Model `Invoice` tracking `invoiceNumber` (sequential `INV-1001`), `businessId`, `customerId`, `subtotal`, `discount`, `taxAmount`, `grandTotal`, `paidAmount`, `balanceDue`, `paymentStatus` (`PAID`, `PARTIAL`, `UNPAID`), `paymentMethod` (`CASH`, `UPI`, `CARD`, `CREDIT`, `SPLIT`), and `status` (`COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`, `REFUNDED`).
    - Compound indexes for multi-tenant query optimization.

[✓] C17. T24: Sale Item Schema Model & Snapshot Preservation (`src/models/saleItem.model.js`)
    - Model `SaleItem` snapshotting `soldPrice`, `costPrice`, `name`, `sku`, `unit`, `quantity`, `totalPrice`, and `grossProfit`.
    - Mathematical guarantee: Future product price changes never alter past transaction invoices.
    - Gross Profit Analytics: `GET /api/sales/analytics/gross-profit` aggregating revenue, COGS, gross margin %, and top-performing items.

[✓] C18. T25: Create Sale (POS Checkout) API with Atomic Transaction Controls (`POST /api/sales`)
    - Pre-Flight Inventory Validation: Validates stock for all cart items before mutations. Rejects with 400 `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` on shortfall.
    - Sequential Invoice Numbering: Generates clean human-readable invoice IDs (`INV-1001`, `INV-1002`).
    - Snapshot Line Items: Bulk inserts `SaleItem` records with frozen prices.
    - Auto Inventory Reduction: Deducts physical inventory and appends immutable `OUT` records to `InventoryLedger`.
    - Payment Linking & Settlement: Records payment and adjusts payment status (`PAID` / `PARTIAL` / `UNPAID`).

[✓] C19. T26: Auto Inventory Deductions & Low-Stock Alerts Sync in Sales Engine
    - Deep integration connecting POS Checkout -> `inventoryService.stockOut` -> `InventoryLedger` -> `inventoryAlertRepository.syncProductAlert`.
    - Deterministically flags low-stock or critical out-of-stock items immediately upon checkout completion.

[✓] C20. T27: Invoice PDF Generation Engine & Thermal Receipts (`GET /api/sales/:id/pdf`)
    - Programmatic PDF generation using `PDFKit` with store branding, GST details, customer info, line item table, totals breakdown, and payment status stamps.
    - Thermal 80mm/58mm printable receipt format support on frontend (`PrintReceiptModal.jsx`).

[✓] C21. T28: Payment Recording Entity & Multi-Tranche Settlement Engine (`/api/payments`)
    - Standalone `Payment` schema (`_id`, `businessId`, `invoiceId`, `customerId`, `amount`, `paymentMethod`, `referenceId`, `status`, `notes`, `receivedAt`).
    - Multi-Tranche Partial Payments: Enables customers to pay invoices across multiple installments (e.g. ₹600 Cash now, ₹400 UPI later).
    - Status Reconciliation: Auto-updates parent `Invoice.paidAmount`, `balanceDue`, and `paymentStatus` (`UNPAID` -> `PARTIAL` -> `PAID`).
    - Dedicated REST APIs:
      * `POST /api/payments`: Record payment against an invoice.
      * `GET /api/payments/invoice/:invoiceId`: Get all payment tranches for an invoice.
      * `GET /api/payments/:id`: Get single payment record.
      * `GET /api/payments/business`: Business-wide payment history query.

[✓] C22. T29: Customer Credit Integration Engine & Khata Ledger (`/api/customers`)
    - Seamlessly connects POS Sales & Invoicing with Customer Credit / Khata (Udhaar) ledger.
    - Automatic Credit Recording: When payment method is `CREDIT` or unpaid balance remains on an invoice, automatically appends a `SALE_CREDIT` transaction to `CustomerLedger`.
    - Customer Master & Balance Tracker: Model `Customer` maintains `totalCredit`, `totalPaid`, and `outstandingBalance`.
    - Khata Settlement Engine (`POST /api/customers/:id/settle`): Records settlement payments (`PAYMENT_RECEIVED`), decrements customer debt, and allocates payment to unpaid invoices.
    - Idempotency & Safety: Deduplication checks prevent double debiting for the same invoice. Credit limit enforcement warnings.
    - Customer APIs:
      * `POST /api/customers`: Create customer profile.
      * `GET /api/customers`: Paginated customer directory with search and balance filters.
      * `GET /api/customers/summary`: KPI strip (Total Customers, Total Udhaar, Active Debtors).
      * `GET /api/customers/:id`: Customer profile details.
      * `PUT /api/customers/:id`: Update customer details & credit limit.
      * `GET /api/customers/:id/ledger`: Paginated statement of all credit/payment transactions.
      * `POST /api/customers/:id/settle`: Settle customer outstanding balance.
      * `GET /api/customers/search`: Fast search by name or mobile number.

[✓] C23. T30: Billing Checkout Front Terminal & POS Screen (`/pos`)
    - Complete POS terminal frontend (`POSTerminalPage.jsx`) designed for rapid keyboard-first cashier workflows:
      * Barcode Scanner Hook (`useBarcodeScanner.js`): Auto-detects hardware barcode scanner input with debounce and automatic cart addition.
      * Global Keyboard Shortcuts (`usePOSKeyboard.js`): `F2` (Search), `F4` (Customer Select), `F8` (Clear Cart), `F9` (Checkout / Pay), `F1` (Help Modal), `Esc` (Close modals).
      * Quick Products Grid: Category pills and 1-click product tile additions.
      * Real-Time Quick Cart (`POSCart.jsx`): Instant quantity adjustment, line discounts, subtotal, tax, and grand total calculations.
      * Customer Selector Modal (`POSCustomerModal.jsx`): Search, select, or quick-add customers inline during checkout.
      * Settle Payment Modal (`SettlePaymentModal.jsx`): Support for CASH, UPI, CARD, and CREDIT/UDHAAR with partial payment splitting and balance due calculation.
      * Print Receipt Modal (`PrintReceiptModal.jsx`): Thermal receipt preview and browser printing.
      * Keyboard Shortcuts Cheat Sheet Modal (`POSKeyboardShortcutsModal.jsx`).
