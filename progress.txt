--- [ PHASE 9 : FINANCIAL ANALYTICS & FULL P&L ENGINES ] ---
[✓] C45. T52: Monthly Revenue Aggregations & Accrual-Basis Financial Analytics Engine (`src/models/monthlyRevenueSummary.model.js`)
    - Official Source of Revenue Truth: Recognizes revenue from completed sales under accrual principles (ignores payment status, strictly excludes cancelled invoices).
    - Cash collected vs accounts receivable segregation, calendar-month UTC alignment, MoM growth variance, and materialized caching in `MonthlyRevenueSummary`.

[✓] C46. T53: Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) Calculator & Historical Cost Snapshot Engine (`src/models/monthlyCogsSummary.model.js`)
    - Mathematical formula: `COGS = SUM(SaleItem.costPrice * SaleItem.quantity)`.
    - Built strictly with frozen `SaleItem.costPrice` snapshots (zero catalog price drift), daily/monthly/period granularities, product cost driver rankings, and materialized caching in `MonthlyCogsSummary`.

[✓] C47. T54: Gross Profit Calculations, GP Margin % & Retail Profitability Engine (`src/models/monthlyGrossProfitSummary.model.js`)
    - Formula: `Gross Profit = Revenue - COGS` and `GP Margin % = (Gross Profit / Revenue) * 100`.
    - Zero-revenue division safety (returns 0% margin without NaN/Infinity).
    - P&L Bridge: `Operating Profit = Gross Profit - OpEx`.
    - Vital Health Diagnostic Indicator: HEALTHY (>=30%), MODERATE (15-30%), LOW (<15%), LOSS (<0%), with MoM margin compression alert (>3% drop).
    - 4-Quadrant Product Profitability Matrix: Star Performers, Volume Drivers, Margin Drag, Loss Making.

[✓] C48. T55: Operating Expense Aggregations & Store Cash Burn Engine (`src/services/opExAnalytics.service.js`)
    - Formula: `Operating Expense = SUM(all valid expenses within financial period)`.
    - Accounting date priority (`expenseDate`), cash burn velocity mapping (daily burn rate, projected monthly burn, annualized run-rate, peak burn days), fixed overhead vs variable cost structure split, and full P&L bridge.

[✓] C49. T56: Net Profit Aggregation Service & Bottom-Line Financial Analytics (`src/models/monthlyNetProfitSummary.model.js`)
    - The ultimate bottom-line financial truth for VendorOS.
    - Mathematical formula: `Net Profit = Gross Profit - Operating Expenses` (or `Revenue - COGS - OpEx`).
    - Net Profit Margin % = `(Net Profit / Revenue) * 100`.
    - Full P&L waterfall cost allocation: `Revenue = COGS + OpEx + Net Profit`.
    - Health ratings: `LOSS`, `BREAK_EVEN`, `SLIM_PROFIT` (<10%), `HEALTHY` (10-20%), `EXCELLENT` (>20%).
    - Materialized cache in `MonthlyNetProfitSummary` collection, quarterly rollups (Q1-Q4), and daily bottom-line ledger.

  * Files:
    - `Backend/src/repositories/sale.repository.js`
    - `Backend/src/repositories/inventory.repository.js`
    - `Frontend/src/features/pos/POSTerminalPage.jsx`
    - `Frontend/src/features/inventory/InventoryAuditPage.jsx`
  * Issue: Calling Mongoose `.save()` on an in-memory document instance after atomic `findOneAndUpdate` overwrote concurrent stock decrements made by other checkout terminals with stale values.
  * Fix:
    - Backend: Replaced `.save()` with targeted `Inventory.updateOne({ _id }, { $set: { lowStockAlert, updatedAt } })`, preserving exact concurrent atomic stock changes.
    - Frontend: Added instant catalog re-fetch (`fetchPOSProducts()`) upon `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` rejection and inventory table sync on conflict error.

- [RESOLVED] BUG 2.3: Sequential Number Generator Race Conditions (Invoice, PO, GRN):
  * Files:
    - `Backend/src/models/counter.model.js` (Created dedicated atomic counter schema)
    - `Backend/src/models/index.js` (Exported Counter model)
    - `Backend/src/repositories/counter.repository.js` (Created centralized atomic sequence generator)
    - `Backend/src/repositories/sale.repository.js` (Integrated atomic getNextInvoiceNumber)
    - `Backend/src/repositories/purchaseOrder.repository.js` (Integrated atomic generateNextPoNumber)
    - `Backend/src/repositories/grn.repository.js` (Integrated atomic generateNextGrnNumber)
    - `Backend/src/services/grn.service.js` (Passed session context to generateNextGrnNumber)
  * Issue: Previous generator calculated next sequential numbers via countDocuments/findOne + in-memory loop check, causing simultaneous POS checkouts or PO/GRN creations to generate identical IDs and crash with MongoServerError E11000 duplicate key error.
  * Fix: Implemented atomic MongoDB `$inc` counter collection with automatic cold-start seeding from existing documents, backward compatibility, and race condition duplicate key recovery.
  * Test: `Backend/tests/test-bug-2.3-sequential-counter-race-fix.js` -> 100% Passed.
